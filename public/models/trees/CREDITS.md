- Two meshes, 2,173 triangles (1,885 foliage + 288 trunk), three embedded PNGs,

- The displayed tree is scaled to 5 m. The original comparison instance and
  detached branch test are no longer displayed.
- The Sheem study additionally changes material roughness/color/normal-map strength,
  uses alpha cutouts for leaves, blends leaf normals toward canopy normals, and
  remaps leaf colors with custom shading. These are Sheem modifications, not
  changes endorsed by the original creator. Geometry silhouette and source textures
  remain from the original work. The source GLB itself is not overwritten.

- Runtime leaf contact deformation and trunk collision are Sheem additions.
  The styled appearance is applied in code; there is no separate baked Sheem GLB.
