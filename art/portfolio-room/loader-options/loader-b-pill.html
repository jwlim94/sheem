Setting up the room0%Scroll ↓
B · Scroll pill
  • The loading lives where “Scroll ↓” will be: the same pill at the bottom, filling with warm light as it loads. When it's ready it shrinks into “Scroll ↓”, so the first thing to do is right where you were looking.
  • The middle of the screen is left to the room (blurred, drifting slowly) and the headline. Nothing new on the page: one control changes what it says.
  • Quietest of the three; the percentage is small, there if you look for it.
What it costs
  • The blurred picture is a tiny thumbnail, under 1 KB (440 bytes for wide screens, 406 for phones), put in the page itself so it shows with the first paint, before anything else loads.
  • One thumbnail per shape of screen (wide, tall) to make again when the room's opening view changes.
During the busy second
  • The slow drift and the dust keep moving (CSS only), so the page never looks stuck; the number and the fill stop for that second.

Replay (or R) to watch it again; 20 Mbps plays the slower load. The orange “page busy” marks the ~1 s when the real page can’t update anything driven by script.
