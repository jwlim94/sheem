# Rabbit character

Status: V64 refinement approved by the user on 2026-09-30. The user selected the rabbit from the
[human/rabbit concept](content/assets/characters/human-rabbit-concepts-v1.png).
V9 adds a local in-place walk study while preserving the V8 appearance.
This is an increment toward the next character-focused Short, not completion of
M1 locomotion or the audio MVP.

## Look and deliverables

Warm oatmeal body, subtle cheek fullness, upright peach-lined ears, sage kerchief, apricot
crossbody satchel and a round tail. Proportions and accessories follow the concept;
the geometry is a first interpretation for review, not an exact reconstruction.

- [Browser GLB](../public/models/rabbit/sheem-rabbit-v64.glb): 2,912,236 bytes, 30,635
  triangles, three material primitives, fifteen bones, a four-second `Idle` and
  0.8-second `Walk` and `Run` clips. One embedded 768×768 face color texture and two fur normal textures (256×256 body, 768×768 head); no external texture or decoder requests.
- [Editable Blender source](../art/characters/rabbit/sheem-rabbit-v64.blend).
- [Rebuild script](../art/characters/rabbit/build_rabbit.py), tested in Blender 4.4.1.
- [Studio render](../art/characters/rabbit/rabbit-preview-v64.png).

The model was authored locally from the in-session generated concept, using
procedural mesh construction in Blender. No third-party character mesh or texture
was downloaded. The script is the reproducible source; rebuilding overwrites the
named `.blend`, `.glb` and preview PNG. Save hand-edited variants separately.

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup --python art/characters/rabbit/build_rabbit.py
npm run dev
```

Open `/playground/rabbit`. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, choose front/side/back,
select Idle/Walk/Run, or pause playback in the studio. Reduced-motion preference
starts the preview paused. The product's `/` route retains its existing title and
entry behavior; the rabbit study is loaded only on its own route.

## Face revision

The initial raised eye meshes looked like buttons from the side. V2 paints the
eyes, highlights, eyebrows and smile directly onto the head using front-projected
UVs. The raised muzzle patch is removed; cheek and muzzle tint are softly painted.
Eyes are slightly smaller and matte. Only the nose retains very shallow relief
(about 6 mm at avatar scale). Back polygons sample plain fur, so the expression
does not appear on the back of the head.

The texture is generated by the modeling script, packed into both Blender and
GLB, and saved as `art/characters/rabbit/rabbit-face-v2.png` for inspection. V1
model files are retained for comparison; the preview and download now use V15.

## Walk study (V9)

The preview now offers Idle, Walk and Pause/Play. Switching while playing blends
poses over 0.25 seconds; choosing a clip while paused displays its starting pose.
Reduced-motion preference starts paused. The accepted V8 geometry is retained.
Two independent foot bones keep soles level and a bag bone adds restrained sway.
Arms counter-swing; body, ears, head and tail have small secondary movements.

The in-place cycle lasts 1.6 seconds. Feet alternate a 0.8-second planted phase
and eased return, with 4.5 cm maximum lift and 20 cm stance travel. A future
controller should match approximately 0.25 m/s at normal playback speed to cancel

rigid-part gait on flat ground, not terrain IK, keyboard locomotion or footstep audio.

### Articulated knees and ankle roll (V15)

Each side now has an upper leg, a child Shin and a child Foot. The leg skin blends
upper/lower weights across the knee. Fixed segment lengths replace the former
thigh scaling; hips sit inside the pear-shaped torso to allow a folded resting
chain without changing the mesh silhouette. The knee bends toward an explicit
forward pole as the foot recovers. Foot orientation moves from a gentle heel
contact through flat support to a toe push (about 18° Walk / 24° Run), then returns
during swing. Rounded sole vertices determine the authored contact height.

The reusable authoring solver is [two_bone_ik.py](../art/rigging/two_bone_ik.py).
It accepts hip/ankle/pole positions and two lengths, clamps unreachable targets
without stretching, and has no rabbit names or gait timings. Tests cover three
proportion sets, opposite knee poles, coincident/unreachable targets and invalid
lengths. Run with Blender:

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup --python art/rigging/test_two_bone_ik.py
```

The runtime [sole clearance helper](../src/sheem/characters/soleClearance.ts)
caches vertices rigidly weighted to named feet. After pose blending it applies a
small whole-avatar vertical correction only when a sole dips below the local
support plane. This prevents start/stop interpolation from cutting into the
floor; it is not independent foot planting on uneven terrain. Names are explicit
parameters so a future human rig can reuse the helper. Human joint locations,
skin weights and gait tuning still need their own authoring pass.

V14 assets are preserved. Speeds and requested forward reach remain unchanged.
Acceptance: compare Side Walk/Run, watching the knee fold on recovery and heel
rise at push-off, then tap/release movement and stop mid-run in the meadow.
A close side-view before/after is suitable for the next character-motion Short.

Validation: solver tests pass for three limb proportions and edge cases. Browser
sampling of 97 poses per clip found segment-length variation below 0.000001 m and
scale variation below 0.000001; knee flexion spans about 39° in Walk and 80° in Run.
Raw clip interpolation can dip the sole by up to 2.3 mm; runtime clearance handles
this and pose-transition dips. Ten Walk/Run release timings, interrupted restart

### Ambient blinking

The browser avatar blinks independently of Idle/Walk/Run. After an initial
1.5–3 second wait, each blink closes over 75 ms, holds for 35 ms and reopens over
120 ms, followed by a randomized 3–6 second wait. Gait changes do not reset it.
Preview Pause and its reduced-motion default freeze blinking with the other
animation. No timers, React frame state or extra eye meshes are introduced.

[rabbitBlink.ts](../src/sheem/characters/rabbitBlink.ts) compresses the painted eye
regions in the V15 face atlas and draws a subtle closed-lid line. It clones only
the mapped face material per instance and restores/disposes that clone on cleanup;
shared geometry and texture remain cached. The clock is independent of the atlas
coordinates. A future character needs its own eyelid representation. This is a
browser material effect; downloading the GLB alone does not include blinking.

Validation: browser renders checked open, half-closed and closed eyes; a seeded
clock ran through varied intervals with values bounded to [0,1]. Lint/build pass
and no browser exceptions were recorded.

## Runtime contract

GLB uses +Y up, +Z forward, feet at approximately zero and a height of approximately 1.69 m
including ears. This is a provisional avatar scale, not the size of a real rabbit.
`RabbitModel` clones skeletons per instance and owns its animation mixer; cached
geometry and vertex-painted materials remain shared. Frame updates do not render
React. Mixer actions/bindings are cleaned up on unmount.

The mesh contains intersecting stylized parts, with blended knee weights and
otherwise mostly rigid part weights. It has
Idle, Walk and Run clips, but not a production facial rig or individual-foot terrain IK. The separate meadow study supplies a bounded movement controller.

The Meadow option reuses the title environment with an eight-meter grass clearing
around the subject so close grass does not obstruct inspection. This is a preview
arrangement, not a change to the title's terrain or a walkable spawn specification.
